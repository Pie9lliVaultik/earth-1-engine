01The accuracy ladder

GlobalOpinionQA: 40 World Values Survey questions × 66 countries (2,598 country-question pairs). Leave-one-country-out cross-validation — the engine predicts each country having seen nothing from it. Lower is better.

*Strongest leakage-free lookup: SVD polarity-aligned country offsets. The comparison is tilted against the engine — the lookup sees the held-out country's answers to other questions; the engine sees nothing from it at all. The engine wins regardless, by 1.2pp across 2,598 pairs.

Three plausible signal sources (news tone, topic salience ×2 statistics) were killed by our own pre-registered placebo tests before reaching production. No competitor publishes equivalent negative results — or equivalent controls.

06Competitive position

Simile, Aaru, and Artificial Societies publish no APIs, no reproducible benchmarks, and no pre-registered validation. Earth-1's position is structural: every claim above traces to a public dataset, a committed seed, and an append-only record. The predecessor system (per-agent LLM simulation, the industry's default architecture) scored 26.4pp on this same benchmark — 2.5× worse than Earth-1's physics engine, at ~1000× the inference cost per query.
