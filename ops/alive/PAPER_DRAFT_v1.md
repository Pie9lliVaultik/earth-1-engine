# 1. The instrument and its guarantees

Earth-1 is a deterministic simulation of a synthetic civilization. This paper does not claim the simulation is right; it claims the apparatus that measures it is honest, and reports what that apparatus has measured so far — misses included, priors quoted where they were written down before the result.

## 1.1 Determinism as the base guarantee

Every claim downstream rests on Block 0: six determinism checks, all passing [source: ops/alive/BLOCK0_DETERMINISM.json]. Three distinct execution paths — same-seed rebirth, save-load continuation, and a run with the distress flag at baseline — produce one identical world hash, `49bb7d1d949f28f3` [source: ops/alive/BLOCK0_DETERMINISM.json]. Branching under common random numbers and historical rebirth hash separately and pass; the adapter determinism check reproduces `p_model = 0.4311624...` exactly [source: ops/alive/BLOCK0_DETERMINISM.json]. Three paths, one hash, is what lets a frozen forecast mean anything: the committed output is the only output that config can produce.

## 1.2 The calibration discipline

Calibration runs as a ledger: one named change per cycle, scored against gates fixed by a locked ruling [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md]. The ledger is mostly misses, recorded as misses — the first full-green cycle was c010, and each prior MISS row carries a name (c004: "NAMED: age"; c005: "NAMED: physics") rather than a workaround [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md]. A VOID verdict is reserved for defects in the measuring instrument itself, never for unwelcome physics results — e.g., the retrodiction dose-suppression finding was VOIDed only after being traced to an entry-metric counting defect [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md]. The discipline is mechanical where it can be: a config-hash guard makes no-op rows unrecordable and an anchors-change guard is armed in the runner [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md]; the comparison floor is computed on frozen WVS-7 DEV cells and weights, invariant to physics changes by construction, and no win counts below 2σ of seed noise [source: BIBLE.md §4.2.2]. The discipline also runs backwards: when cells and weights were frozen by ruling, an earlier apparent attitude improvement was rescored and retracted — c003→c009→c010 moved 12.00→11.88→11.97 pp, noise, and the model's cohort-structure deviations measured worse than predicting none (3.88–3.97 vs 2.571) [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md]. Two v4.2 rulings bound what can ever be claimed: no comparison against prompted frontier LLMs anywhere, and no unspent WVS holdout exists — WVS-7 is calibration-only, forever, with external estates judged under a wave-split rule (latest wave sealed, prior waves validation) [source: BIBLE.md §4.2.1–4.2.2].

## 1.3 Frozen-forecast-then-score

For a resolved historical event, the protocol is: freeze the model's output before any outcome data is fetched, then fetch the judge once, then score once — no reruns. The first full execution was the 2022 Ukraine food shock: output committed at 200k agents × 16 seeds × 180 days under the registered dose, then the GRFC judge fetched post-freeze (sha 44de7226, 48 countries) [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md]. The score was three losses: magnitude 8.5× over (+326M vs +38.5M undernourished), geography ρ = −0.137, top-mover overlap 1/5 [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md]. The founder's written prior predicted all three, including Egypt's zero delta (the bread-subsidy damper); each loss became a named mechanism cycle [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md]. Historical rebirth is itself guarded: `birth(year=T)` mechanically asserts no input, anchor, or news item postdates T [source: BIBLE.md §v4.2.2 refinement 10].

## 1.4 The sealed judge

Frozen-forecast-then-score still lets a team learn from a judge it has already opened. The sealed-judge protocol closes that: for the 2010–11 food-price spike, the outcome estate (FAO SOFI undernourishment changes, WFP/FEWS 2011 assessments) is registered by name and location only — no value from those sources may enter any calibration, transfer function, or conversation before the model's 2010 output is frozen; the fetch happens once [source: ops/alive/FOOD2010_SEALED_PREREG.md]. Metrics are pre-committed (within 2× on scaled magnitude; Spearman ρ > 0.3; top-5 overlap ≥ 2/5), and this judge fits nothing [source: ops/alive/FOOD2010_SEALED_PREREG.md]. The seal is hardened at the parser level: a bulk file already on disk for exposure-side work contains the sealed items, so every parser touching it whitelists one item code, and ingesting any undernourishment value before the freeze VOIDs the judge [source: ops/alive/FOOD2010_SEALED_PREREG.md]. The result slot in this paper is deliberately empty: **[SEALED — forecast frozen, judge unspent]**. That the slot exists before the number does is the instrument's argument.

## 1.5 What freeze-0.9 froze, and what it admitted

The physics froze on 2026-09-01 as freeze-with-named-regression: Benchmark B DEV direction was 40% against an 80% gate, and the tag carries that failure plus five named regressions rather than a fix that would have reopened the loop [source: ops/alive/FREEZE_PACKAGE_0_9.md]. The frozen board shows hits and reds side by side: poverty at $8.30 within 0.2 pp of the fetched value and age-at-death at band center, but the $3.00 tail 1.6× over and unemployment ~9.7% vs 4.8% [source: ops/alive/FREEZE_PACKAGE_0_9.md]. The freeze also registered a sizing finding against itself: a 200k world warmed on 90 days of real news ticks at ~4–6× the cost of a cold world (2h43m+ vs ~35–40m CPU per 90-day run; 7.3 GB vs ~2.5 GB RSS), traced to per-memory chronicle spread — recorded as a pre-Epoch-4 requirement, not an optimization note [source: ops/alive/FREEZE_PACKAGE_0_9.md].

## 2. What the population gets right and wrong (opinion distance)

The core question this instrument answers is not "does Earth-1 win" but "how far is a synthetic population from what real people actually said." The measurement protocol is fixed before reading any result: frozen engine (0.9 plus adopted flags), 200k agents, three world seeds, leave-one-country-out scoring, and named-entity abstention on — the engine declines questions about people and institutions its world has never heard of, and those 2,198 abstained cells are excluded from both sides of the comparison rather than silently counted as hits or misses [source: ops/alive/DISTANCE_FROM_HUMANS_2026-09-02.md].

**Three numbers at three levels of strictness.** At the coarsest bar — does the model land on the same side as the real human majority — agreement is 83.5% across 21,776 scored (item, country) cells [source: ops/alive/DISTANCE_FROM_HUMANS_2026-09-02.md]. Against the strictest bar — could a pollster distinguish the model's answer from the survey's own 95% sampling noise — only 14.2% of the 12,669 cells with known survey n pass [source: ops/alive/DISTANCE_FROM_HUMANS_2026-09-02.md]. The typical answer sits 3.78 sampling-errors from the human number, an MAE of 10.65 percentage points [source: ops/alive/DISTANCE_FROM_HUMANS_2026-09-02.md]. Stated plainly: the population usually agrees with humanity's majority and is rarely yet as precise as a real poll [source: ops/alive/DISTANCE_SUMMARY_PLAIN.md]. Signed error is near zero overall (−0.04pp on WVS held-out, −0.0 on WVS extended) — the misses are not one global bias but family-specific tilts [source: ops/alive/DISTANCE_FROM_HUMANS_2026-09-02.md].

**Against the pre-registered bar and informed baselines, this is a miss.** The registered acceptance tier was MAE ≤7.0pp [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_FULL_v1.md]. On the WVS held-out estate the national readout scores 11.84pp against baselines of 11.18 (MrsP), 12.80 (naive) and 9.70 (region-copy): Earth-1 beats only the naive baseline [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_FULL_v1.md]. At item level it beats region-copy on 7 of 98 held-out WVS items (7%), 17 of 110 extended items (15%), and 137 of 468 Pew-frame items (29%) [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_ITEMS_v1.md]; the per-item numbers are computed with the same loaders as the committed aggregates and reconcile exactly, with raw tables at data/cycles/sb1_items_{wvs_heldout,wvs_extended,goqa_dev}.{json,csv} [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_ITEMS_v1.md].

**Where it wins.** The gains cluster where the engine has a real substrate. Interpersonal and outgroup trust: trust-other-religion (−1.2pp vs region-copy), trust-strangers (−1.2), trust-other-nationality (−1.1) — carried by the social-graph and force substrate [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_ITEMS_v1.md]. Material hardship: gone-without-cash-income (−0.9pp), carried by the economics substrate [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_ITEMS_v1.md]. And Pew event-era items with high within-region variance — Iraq-war-era judgments (−8.6, −7.9, −7.3pp) and Islam-in-politics (−7.4) — where regional averaging fails and country covariates win [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_ITEMS_v1.md]. In the distance framing, the closest family is tech/climate (2.97σ) and the closest region Oceania (2.28σ) [source: ops/alive/DISTANCE_FROM_HUMANS_2026-09-02.md].

**Where it loses, and why.** The worst losses name their own missing inputs. Religious belief is the single worst family (3.91σ) [source: ops/alive/DISTANCE_FROM_HUMANS_2026-09-02.md]: religion-exclusivism (+7.8pp vs region-copy), belief-in-hell (+7.5), afterlife (+5.5) — no religiosity input exists in genesis, and region-copy wins because religion is regionally clustered [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_ITEMS_v1.md]. Geopolitical favorability is the worst single miss: the Pew "EU understands you" item at +25.7pp and US-impact items at +9 to +16pp — no geopolitical-alignment input exists [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_ITEMS_v1.md]. Regionally, East Asia is farthest (4.66σ) [source: ops/alive/DISTANCE_FROM_HUMANS_2026-09-02.md]. Each gap maps to a named, fetchable input: national religiosity (Pew Religion), geopolitical alignment (UN voting-similarity), and institutional-confidence levels (V-Dem) [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_ITEMS_v1.md]. That the loss map converts to a shopping list rather than an excuse is a property of the instrument, not a promise about the inputs.

**The verdict replicates on items the pipeline never chose.** After a founder challenge ("the WVS doesn't have only 141 questions"), 110 additional substantive WVS items were found in the raw respondent data — never labeled, never calibrated on, never seen by any tuning step — registered under the same frozen rules and scored under the identical protocol [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_FULL_v1.md]. Result: 11.20 ± 0.16pp against MrsP 10.43, naive 11.33, region-copy 9.81 — the same verdict, beats naive only, ~0.8pp behind MrsP and ~1.4pp behind region-copy [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_FULL_v1.md]. The replication settles that the A-board verdict is not an item-selection artifact; total judged WVS items now stand at 208 [source: ops/alive/BENCHMARK_A_FULL_v1.md]. A cleaner judge produced the same sentence, which is exactly what an honest instrument should do.

# 3. The named finding: material stress predicts unrest geography

If this instrument has one finding worth naming, it is this: where the engine's material-stress fields go, real unrest geography tends to follow — and where its fear fields go, it does not. The finding was registered as the campaign's most robust result, converging "from four independent directions" [source: ops/alive/PUBLICATION_PREREG_v1.md]. This section walks the four legs. None of them was tuned on any other; two of them are misses.

**Leg 1 — mechanism (c-SHOCK).** A shock-response cycle on the 0.9 candidate physics asked which causal channels actually carry a dose through the world. The per-event direction table splits cleanly: every pure-economic direction passes (GFC 2008: jobs, poverty, hope all correct), while every force-threshold-mediated direction fails (Arab Spring governments and displacement, COVID hope and deaths) [source: ops/alive/cycles/cshock.md]. When the probe was rebuilt with event-time counting, the graded-hardship world transmitted a sustained protest dose into sustained unrest — +2,167 hot-locality-days versus +300 for the old cliff physics at 200k — while the cliff-tuned protest_risk gate absorbed almost everything (+7 vs +282) [source: ops/alive/cycles/cshock.md]. The cycle also voided its own earlier dose-suppression sub-finding as an instrument defect (metric semantics), which we record here because a paper about honesty should show the instrument catching itself [source: ops/alive/cycles/cshock.md].

**Leg 2 — a real event, scored against a sealed prior (UKR-2022).** The 2022 food-price spike was run as a registered 20% global trade shock, model output frozen before any outcome data was fetched [source: ops/alive/UKR2022_FOOD_PREREG.md]. The model moved: hungry +8,040.5 agents (±99.3), world-scaled ~+326M [source: ops/alive/consequences/ukr2022_food.json]. The judge (GRFC AFI database, 48 countries) said +38.5M (191.4M→230.0M): **M1 FAIL at 8.5×** [source: ops/alive/consequences/ukr2022_judge_scored.json; ops/alive/UKR2022_FOOD_PREREG.md]. **M2 FAIL**: geographic Spearman ρ = −0.137 (p=0.37, n=44), scored — a registered caveat — on force-shift ranking, not hunger ranking [source: ops/alive/UKR2022_FOOD_PREREG.md]. **M3: 1/5** top-mover overlap (Somalia); the real top movers were UA, ET, NG, PK, SO — a war-and-drought surge with price as amplifier [source: ops/alive/UKR2022_FOOD_PREREG.md]. The founder's prior, recorded verbatim before the judge was fetched, predicted exactly this shape: "M2/M3 likely to miss, and the miss is the more useful result," naming missing damper channels (substitution, subsidies, stock drawdown) and the absent food-import-dependence input [source: ops/alive/UKR2022_FOOD_PREREG.md]. Reality then handed back the calibration target the prior asked for: **Egypt's real hunger delta is 0.0** — the bread subsidy absorbed the shock, the named damper confirmed by its own absence [source: ops/alive/consequences/ukr2022_judge_scored.json; ops/alive/UKR2022_FOOD_PREREG.md]. The miss belongs to this finding because of what failed and what did not: the material response was large, real, and correctly signed; the geography failed for a nameable material reason (poverty geography emerged where food-exposure geography was needed) [source: ops/alive/UKR2022_FOOD_PREREG.md].

**Leg 3 — the consequence battery.** Across five registered scenarios, material shocks resolve where institutional ones abstain. The MENA food-spike report resolves 7 lines, led by hungry +103.7 agents (±26.1), with Egypt the top country mover (0.0343) [source: ops/alive/consequences/d_food_spike_mena.json]; the rate-hike resolves 8 [source: ops/alive/consequences/a_rate_hike_us.json]. The contested-election scenario resolves 3 lines against 16 abstentions [source: ops/alive/consequences/c_contested_election_br.json]. Stated plainly: the earthquake scenario also resolves only 3 of 21 [source: ops/alive/consequences/e_earthquake_megacity_jp.json] — the engine speaks where it has a material transmission channel and abstains where it does not, which is the point.

**Leg 4 — the retrodiction that names the section.** Arab Spring, birthed at T=2010-12-16, output frozen, then judged against real GDELT protest events across 185 countries: the hunger field predicts real protest geography at **ρ=0.168 (p=0.022)**; the fear field does not (ρ=0.069, p=0.35, ns) [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md]. The model's material-stress ordering put Egypt first [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md]. The signal is weak — ρ=0.17 explains little variance, magnitude was qualitatively under (a region-wide uprising versus +489 hot-locality-days), and the judge carries a named media-density bias (US ranked #1 on raw GDELT counts) [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md]. But it is significant, it is carried by hunger and not fear, and it points the same direction as the other three legs.

**The claim, sized honestly.** Four instruments — a mechanism probe, a sealed-prior counterfactual, a synthetic battery, a historical retrodiction — agree that the engine's material channels are where its predictive content lives, and its fear/institutional channels are where its misses live. The claim is directional, not calibrated: the one significant correlation is 0.168, and the one real-units magnitude test failed by 8.5×.

**The test this section must eventually pass.** A second food-price spike, 2010–11, is registered as this family's claim judge: exposure sources named, outcome estate sealed on disk under a binding parser whitelist, gates pre-committed (M1 within 2×, M2 ρ>0.3, M3 ≥2/5) [source: ops/alive/FOOD2010_SEALED_PREREG.md]. Its result line in this paper is, deliberately, still empty:

> 2010–11 food-price spike: **[SEALED — forecast frozen, judge unspent]**

That this slot exists before the number does is the argument.

# 4. The historical battery and the sealed slot

The battery is twelve real events, registered in one file with priors written before any run [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_EVENTS_v1.json]. The protocol is fixed: the world is born at time T on archive news, the forecast is frozen, the judge data is fetched afterward, and scoring happens in one pass — no reruns after the judge fetch; misses become named engineering cycles, not deleted rows [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_EVENTS_v1.json]. Leakage guards are mechanical: a vintage assertion on every source at birth(T), a news-dated-≤-T check, no national input derived from an estate that also serves as judge; a violation voids the event [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_EVENTS_v1.json]. Four rows are scored.

**Arab Spring (T=2010-12-16).** The committed prior: "geography partially right (protest-geo rho~0.55 lineage); magnitude under (injector thin); govs weak" [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_EVENTS_v1.json]. Against real GDELT protest events for 2010-12-17 to 2011-03-16 across 185 countries, the hunger field correlates with real protest geography at Spearman +0.168 (p=0.022) globally and +0.372 (p=0.16) on the 16-country MENA subset; the fear field does worse: +0.069 (p=0.35) [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md; ops/alive/historical/arab_spring_scored.json]. The magnitude miss is plain: a region-wide uprising against a modeled +489 hot-locality-days [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md]. The surprise the row records is that material stress, not fear, carries the signal [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md]. The judge itself was found biased — the US ranks first in raw counts with 19.5k events on media density — so from event 3 onward GDELT judges are normalized by country total event volume, with the raw table reported beside the normalized one, forward-only and never retroactive [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md; ops/alive/HISTORICAL_EVENTS_v1.json].

**GFC (T=2008-09-14, 200k).** Prior: "jobs direction right, low significance (t=+0.7 at 5 reps; 16 seeds may lift)" [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_EVENTS_v1.json]. Against the World Bank series (real 2008→2009 world delta +0.585pp), the model produced Δunemployed +449 ± 203 agents (t=2.2), about +0.37pp of the labor frame — a magnitude ratio of 0.64×, within the 2× gate; the seed lift materialized (t 0.7 → 2.2) [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md]. The hope observable still carries its KNOWN-DEFECT stamp (wrong sign, t=+2.1 in DEV), reported as such [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md].

**COVID (T=2020-02-28, 200k).** Against real +0.999pp, the model gave +4,177 ± 230 agents (t≈18), about +3.5pp — 3.5× over, within the 5× band, direction emphatic; the frame caveat (real furloughs hid inside the u-rate; the model has no furlough state) was carried forward from DEV, not invented after the fact [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md]. Deaths are absent because the model has no epidemic channel — exactly as the prior said [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_EVENTS_v1.json; ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md].

**SVB (T=2023-03-08, 200k).** Prior: "UNDER-contagion expected: no interbank network channel; p_model likely < market" [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_EVENTS_v1.json]. The frozen forecast put p=0.302 on contagion to ≥2 further banks within 30 days; it resolved YES (Signature Bank, 2023-03-12), and the market had priced contagion higher [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md; ops/alive/historical/svb_2023_frozen.json]. The frozen payload shows the shape of the miss: 17 of 22 observables ABSTAIN — every material line — while the US is the top force mover at 0.0356, roughly twice the runner-up [source: ops/alive/historical/svb_2023_frozen.json]. Right about the fear, missing the plumbing; the interbank/deposit mechanism for v1.1 was registered with the prior, before the score [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_BATTERY_v1.md].

Every frozen payload carries a tier table labeling each observable ABSTAIN, UNCALIBRATED, or KNOWN-DEFECT: GFC reads 11/8/3, SVB 2/17/3, Truss 1/18/3 [source: ops/alive/historical/gfc_2008_frozen.json; ops/alive/historical/svb_2023_frozen.json; ops/alive/historical/truss_2022_frozen.json]. Truss abstained on its own forecast with a stated reason — "all branch deltas below class noise floor 0.02498 (branch = control)" [source: ops/alive/historical/truss_2022_frozen.json]. Because an abstention at 20k can be lack of power rather than physics, the promotion rule sends any significant 20k result — and any abstention on a single-country question — to 200k for confirmation; only physics abstentions (branch ≈ control at full fidelity) stand as answers; truss_2022 and sri_lanka_2022 were promoted under it [source: ops/alive/HISTORICAL_EVENTS_v1.json].

One row of the battery is deliberately empty. The 2010–11 food-price spike is the claim judge for the food-shock family: the 2022 case is opened and therefore DEV — fix on it, never claim on it [source: ops/alive/FOOD2010_SEALED_PREREG.md]. The exposure series (FAO Food Price Index, June 2010 → February 2011) is DEV-usable; the outcome estate — SOFI prevalence-of-undernourishment changes 2010→2011 by country — is registered by name and location only, unfetched, with pre-committed gates: scaled Δhungry within 2×, per-country Spearman ρ > 0.3, top-5 overlap ≥ 2/5 [source: ops/alive/FOOD2010_SEALED_PREREG.md]. The seal is physically enforced: a bulk file already on disk contains the sealed items, so every parser touching it whitelists a single exposure item code, and ingesting any undernourishment value before the freeze voids the judge [source: ops/alive/FOOD2010_SEALED_PREREG.md].

**[SEALED — forecast frozen, judge unspent]**

The slot precedes the number because the slot is the claim. A score reported after the fact can always have been selected; a gate committed before the fetch cannot. Everything that makes the four scored rows legible — priors that named the misses before they happened, tier stamps that abstain rather than guess, a normalization rule that only applies forward — converges in this empty cell: the instrument states, in public, what it will accept as failure before it knows the answer. Whatever number eventually fills the slot, the fact that the slot existed first is what makes that number evidence.

## 5. Learning, the living world, and the product surface

**The engine learns from experience, and the gates were built to catch it pretending to.** Experience Loop v0.2 ran twenty worlds with sharp shocks and a filter arm added as a founder condition [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md, v0.2 row]. The gate that matters most is the one designed to kill the result: G1c compares the experiential learner against an *informed non-learner* — a particle filter given the same observations but forbidden to update its physics. The learner beat it by +1.51 log-score [1.21, 1.81], p=3e-5, positive in 16 of 16 well-specified worlds; late-window error was 0.216 against the filter's 1.04, a 4.8x gap [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md, v0.2 row]. The supporting gates: +2.16 over a frozen model (p=3.1e-5), +0.55 over a naive updater [0.32, 0.77] (p=2.1e-4), +0.59 inside shock windows (p=6.1e-5), 90% intervals covering at 0.963, recovery u-error 0.006/0.034, and 4/4 honest-uncertainty checks under deliberate misspecification [source: data/cycles/v02_report.json]. The causal control holds in both directions: a placebo learner is statistically indistinguishable from frozen (Δ=0.078, p=0.90) [source: data/cycles/v02_report.json], while the real learner beats the placebo by +2.08 (p=3e-5) [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md, v0.2 row]. Two caveats stated as recorded: the stage scorer omitted the filter arm, so G1c and G7′ were computed directly from the arm files by the same paired method, with a scorer patch owed; and the cycle closed only when G6 replay came back bit-identical [source: ops/alive/CALIBRATION_CYCLES.md, v0.2 row].

**The living world is observable without being touchable.** The event wire publishes individual moments — job_lost, job_found, partnership, firm_failed, firm_opened, protest_onset, birth, death, eviction, migration — into an append-only, hash-chained JSONL, with each moment naming at most 12 agents ("a moment names a few people, never a census") [source: earth1/eventwire.py]. The design rule is observation-only: the wire draws no randomness and writes no world state, so an instrumented world and an uninstrumented one must be bitwise identical [source: earth1/eventwire.py, docstring]. That claim is enforced by a gate, not a promise: same seed, 20k agents, 30 days, flag on versus flag off, and the two world hashes must match exactly [source: scripts/blocks/eventwire_gate.py]. The gate ships in the same commit as the wire itself (7999e2b, 2026-09-01), so the collector has never existed in the repository without its own refusal condition [source: git log 7999e2b].

**The product surface is deliberately narrow.** Six endpoints over frozen 0.9 physics: ask, consequences, forecast lookup, world state, world history, health — typed in an OpenAPI 3.1 spec [source: ops/alive/API_V1_README.md; ops/alive/API_V1.yaml]. The contract is enforced in code: every response carries epoch, freeze tag, tree hash, and ledger cutoff; `p_model` is the only scored field, and market prices are displayed but never scored; branch runs happen on copies, so no request can touch a production epoch or any sealed HOLDOUT or PROSPECTIVE file [source: ops/alive/API_V1_README.md]. Known gaps are registered on the surface itself rather than hidden behind it: adm1 resolution is a registered limitation, and date-range history is pending the recorder store [source: ops/alive/API_V1_README.md].

**Custom models keep user worlds out of the civilization.** A user-owned population model is three physically separate layers: the Earth-1 core, which is engine-owned and never mutated; a per-agent context overlay of person-aligned arrays that sit beside the world, never in it, each attribute carrying provenance and a synthetic flag; and temporary scenario state, dosed through the Memory channel and never persisted unless the owner promotes it [source: earth1/models.py, docstring]. The overlay never enters world state, so Block-0 determinism holds; context sources pass the same provenance discipline as national inputs, meaning a judged estate can never feed a model attribute; and a model's outcomes are stamped UNCALIBRATED until its own resolved outcomes exist [source: earth1/models.py, docstring].

That last stamp is the whole product philosophy in miniature. Every line of every answer the API returns carries a calibration tier — CALIBRATED, UNCALIBRATED, ABSTAIN, or KNOWN-DEFECT — and ABSTAIN lines never carry numbers [source: ops/alive/API_V1_README.md]. The instrument does not sell confidence it has not earned: settled facts come with a source, belief-driven outcomes come stamped with how well calibrated that question class has proven to be, and where the engine cannot answer honestly, it says so in the same typed field as everything else [source: ops/alive/API_V1_README.md]. An answer without its stamp cannot leave the building.
