## The intervention (recorded, existing mechanisms only)

A recession in India: at branch day 0 the weakest 30% of India's
29,704 firms (8,911 firms) close via `life_tick`'s own layoff
transition — 116,302 workers lose employment, firm, tenure — with
employment ties severed through the 0.0d re-homing machinery
(dedicated RNG; the paired main stream stays aligned). Surviving
Indian firms are depressed to health 0.20, below the 0.25 hiring
gate (~12 days to reopen by the canonical recovery drift). Closed
firms sit at 0.05.

## The causal chain, as the state actually recorded it

    firm closures (exogenous, day 0)
      → job loss: −24.75pp employment, immediate            [t≈∞]
      → income loss at the zero-buffer margin: +0.93pp go
        FULLY destitute on day 1 (Δdeprivation ≡ Δdestitute:
        the newly deprived are exactly the hand-to-mouth
        fraction; the buffered majority absorb the shock)     [t=217]
      → wealth buffers drain in days 1–5 (−0.005→−0.011,
        clean while trajectories still track)                [early]
      → psychological/social erosion: mental −0.0005,
        relationship −0.0002 by day 30, both monotone and
        GROWING at horizon end                               [t≈11]
      → collective affect: FEAR +0.0022 within a day,
        sustained — detectable even against the 0.978
        saturation ceiling                                   [t=10.6]
      → opinion: NO pole movement — the fear pole share was
        already ~saturated; the opinion channel cannot
        register marginal fear in this world state           [dead]

Opinion did NOT change, and the trajectory shows exactly why: the
mediating force channel is pinned at its ceiling — the same
saturation the 0.8 queue already carries. The chain is coherent up
to precisely the point where the known 0.8 pathology begins.

## Controls

- Paired control arm: every number above is scenario − control.
- Negative-control population (China, ~1M LF): employment
  −0.06pp±0.03 (t=1.8) at day 15 — a suggestive spillover, NOT
  claimed at N=8; deprivation and fear flat (t≤0.7).
- Broken-path placebo (identical code path, shock aimed at Bhutan:
  5 firms, 52 layoffs): India shows no effect beyond chaos noise
  (employment −0.15pp±0.14 vs main-arm −22.6pp — 150× smaller and
  CI-consistent with zero; deprivation +0.0001). Effects flow
  through the targeted mechanism, not from the act of intervening.

## Dead, missing, or unexpectedly weak paths (findings, kept)

1. **Opinion pole is unreachable** while FEAR sits at its ceiling —
   0.8 diagnostic, third independent confirmation.
2. **Addiction and evictions do not respond in 30 days.** Eviction
   requires arrears accumulation (longer horizon or the rent
   mechanics under-couple to job loss); addiction has no visible
   income/mental coupling at this timescale. Both deserve 0.8/1.x
   scrutiny.
3. **No delayed destitution wave.** Buffer exhaustion did not push
   additional workers into destitution over the month — the entire
   deprivation response is the day-1 zero-buffer fraction, and it
   *shrinks* as re-employment outpaces buffer burn. Either Indian
   buffers are long (wealth ≈ days-of-survival units, mean ~70) or
   the deprivation update under-weights slow depletion. Flagged.
4. **Cohort-mean wealth is a bad instrument** past chaos onset
   (±0.4 noise vs −0.01 signal by day 15); distributional wealth
   statistics should replace it in future probes.
5. Firm-failure hazard and separations are **annual-timescale**
   (pilot 1): endogenous firm distress produces slow-motion
   recessions only; sharp downturns require closure-scale events.

## Verdict

Earth-1 computes a coherent, persistent, mechanism-traceable
causal cascade from a legible economic shock: correct sign and
ordering, plausible recovery timescale, immediate effects
concentrated exactly where theory says (the zero-buffer poor),
slow-burn psychological and social erosion, measurable collective
affect, clean placebo, and honest dead paths that map one-to-one
onto the already-queued 0.8 pathologies. The civilization produces
meaningful counterfactual consequences.
