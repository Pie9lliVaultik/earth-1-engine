# 0.8 ITERATION 3 — the propagation operator (pre-registered)

Founder ruling after IT2: propagation contraction is the strongest
DEMONSTRATED BINDING MECHANISM — not yet "root cause"; this
experiment must try to falsify that conclusion. Conviction's ratchet
remains an independent, real defect on the ledger. Frozen BEFORE any
candidate result exists.

## The three hypotheses this design must distinguish

A. MAGNITUDE — the operator's form is fine; its effective daily
   contraction is simply too strong (η, layers).
B. STRUCTURE — unconditional averaging/pole expansion across every
   social tie inevitably homogenizes any connected population.
C. SCHEDULING — individually plausible interactions become
   pathological because the full-network operator runs
   deterministically twice every simulated day (interaction
   frequency conflated with psychological effect size).

## Literature-derived magnitude constraints (registered now)

- Per-exposure influence: the MODAL response to a peer opinion is no
  change; when movement occurs, compromise weight ≈ 0.3 for FACTUAL
  judgments (Moussaïd et al. 2013 influence map); attitudes move far
  less per exposure.
- Sustained persuasion (the strongest known interventions: deep
  canvassing, saturation advertising) shifts attitudes ~0.05–0.15 SD
  over weeks-to-months → routine daily social exposure must impart
  ≪ 0.02 SD/day of directed attitude movement at population scale.
- Population variance is empirically stable over months absent
  events (registry T5): the social operator's NET daily variance
  contraction must be ≈ 0 (registered bound: ≤ 2%/day net).
- Earth-1 incumbent, for contrast: η = 0.18 × 2 layers of
  certainty-weighted consensus toward neighborhood poles, every
  agent, every day — a per-day influence weight exceeding the
  strongest known persuasion technology, applied universally.

## Candidate arms (all experimental patches; production untouched;
conviction = INCUMBENT law in every cell so the propagation effect
is isolated — see Scope note)

- P1 magnitude (tests A): η ∈ {0.05, 0.02, 0.005} × layers ∈ {1, 2}.
- P2 structure (tests B): bounded-confidence gate — influence only
  from neighbors within per-channel distance δ ∈ {0.3, 0.5};
  crossed with pole-expansion ON vs OFF (value-averaging toward
  neighbor MEANS rather than poles), at incumbent η.
- P3 scheduling (tests C): stochastic exposure — each agent
  processes social influence with daily probability p ∈ {0.1, 0.3}
  at incumbent η/layers (influence accumulates through exposure
  instead of a deterministic daily contraction).
- C0 incumbent reference (must fail — pathological anchor).

## Known-answer control arms (the instrument must detect all five)

- KA1 extreme consensus: η=0.5, layers=4 → must fail fast and hard.
- KA2 zero influence: η=0 → must fail (T7: no cross-person
  transmission).
- KA3 excessive gating: δ=0.05 → must fail (frozen/fragmented
  population, no meaningful transmission between differing agents).
- KA4 fast-mixing graph: incumbent law on a degree-preserving
  RANDOMIZED graph → contraction-per-pass must measurably increase
  (detects graph-mixing sensitivity of the instrument).
- KA5 diversity-source check: incumbent law with lived-target pull
  DISABLED (relax=0) → variance collapse must accelerate vs C0,
  demonstrating the instrument sees the pull's diversity injection.

## Instrumentation (every cell; per-pass hooks inside propagate)

Per layer, per day: force variance before/after (contraction ratio
per pass, cumulative daily), mean local-neighbor distance,
neighborhood unanimity share, pole occupancy; plus the IT2
homogenization panel every 10 days (α distribution + lag vs
unanimity, averaging weight, within/between-country variance) and
the day-90 paired tau fork. 200k no-news worlds, 120 days, shared
seed, relax at canonical 0.25 everywhere except KA5.

## Registered acceptance and failure

Cell-level criteria: the six IT2 criteria (tau band [5,15]d with
residual [0.2,0.6]; α heterogeneity; sd ratio ≥ 0.5 with saturation
< 20%; averaging alive; no railing trend; unanimity < 50%) PLUS:
- net social variance contraction ≤ 2%/day (registered above);
- mean per-day |Δf| attributable to the social operator ≤ 0.02 in
  population-SD units;
- cross-person transmission nonzero (a seeded local opinion cluster
  must measurably spread to immediate neighbors within 30 days).

Registered failure interpretations:
- weakened propagation that freezes everyone independently → FAIL
  (that is KA2's signature, not a fix);
- gating that stops all meaningful transmission → FAIL (KA3);
- a cell that passes only because relax injects diversity against a
  still-pathological operator → FAIL: every passing cell re-runs
  with relax=0.05 and must keep variance collapse < 2×
  (the operator itself must be non-pathological).

## Scope note (registered expectation)

IT3 is primarily an ATTRIBUTION experiment (A vs B vs C). Because
conviction runs the incumbent ratchet in all cells, full registry
acceptance (heterogeneous α, no absorbing state) may be unreachable
here even for a healthy propagation law; if a propagation candidate
fixes homogenization but α still rails, the registered next step is
a joint IT4 (propagation winner × conviction candidates) — NOT a
reinterpretation of IT3 cells. A cell that passes everything
outright short-circuits IT4.
