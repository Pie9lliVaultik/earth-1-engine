## Instrumentation per cell (the homogenization panel, every 10 days)

α mean/sd/deciles, frac>0.99, frac<0.05; per-channel between-person
sd and saturation shares; mean neighbor distance |f − nbmean|;
share of agents with agreement > 0.95 (unanimous neighborhoods);
averaging-term magnitude mean(1−α); within-country and
between-country force variance; push-vs-pull potentials
(mean |nbmean − f| vs mean |target − f|); plus the day-90 tau curve.
These answer directionality: does conviction rail because forces
homogenize, or do forces homogenize because conviction rails — or
both.

## Registered cell-acceptance criteria (ALL required)

1. tau: half-life ∈ [5, 15] days AND day-30 residual ∈ [0.2, 0.6]
   (T1 on the joint system);
2. α at day 120: mean ∈ [0.2, 0.9], sd ≥ 0.05, frac>0.99 < 20%,
   frac at floor < 20% (heterogeneous, neither railed nor frozen);
3. forces at day 120: per-channel sd ≥ 50% of genesis sd AND
   saturation shares < 20% per channel (T5);
4. averaging alive: median (1−α) ≥ 0.10 (T7);
5. no-railing trend: saturation shares non-increasing over days
   90→120 within noise (T5 trajectory form);
6. unanimity bounded: share of agreement>0.95 neighborhoods < 50%
   at day 120 (homogenization check).

Known-answer requirements: every A0 row must FAIL criterion 2;
every B4 column must FAIL criterion 1 (t½ > 15d); B0 rows are
expected to FAIL criterion 1 (t½ ≈ 2d) — if any B0 cell passes
criterion 1 the instrument is broken and the run is VOID.

mechanism from the homogenization panel; if forces homogenize
independently of α and relax, the force-propagation law itself is
promoted to the next registered hypothesis — a third structural
defect, named, not hidden in tuning.
