## 3. Classification against the three outcomes

The contamination is not confined to the opinion layer. It sits in
the trait substrate (bimodal), three years of material trajectories
(wealth, durables, deprivation → 114k WANT deaths), and the
migration/diaspora record. These are histories, not recomputable
projections: you cannot un-die the destitution deaths whose timing
was fear-conditioned, un-drift a bimodal trait field, or un-migrate
50× diaspora growth by swapping the conviction equation.

Assessment: **broad persistent contamination → the corrected physics
cannot validly continue from the existing canonical state.** The
defensible deployment shape is:

    fix accepted on evidence (battery + probe retest)
      → new canonical world initialized under corrected physics
        (Epoch 2), grown to product maturity
      → the current world PRESERVED, journaled, and demoted to
        "pathological reference era" — scientifically valuable
        precisely because its pathology is now fully characterized

A middle path (targeted repair: reset α to a trait-anchored prior,
re-draw traits from genesis law, keep material state) is technically
possible but scientifically indefensible: it would graft a healthy
psychology onto a body whose material history that psychology never
produced. Recommend against unless the founder weighs product
continuity above scientific coherence.
