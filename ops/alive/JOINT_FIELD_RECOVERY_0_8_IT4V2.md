# 0.8 IT4-v2 — JOINT FIELD RECOVERY (pre-registered)

Founder ruling: the two-factor IT4 was superseded BEFORE execution —
IT3 proved canonical relax=0.25 alone makes T1 unreachable, so that
design was structurally incapable of passing. This registration
replaces the hypothesis space; the empirical registry (288e37b), the
outcome targets, the known-answer philosophy, and every acceptance
threshold remain frozen and unchanged. Smallest defensible repair
first: weaker push + slower evidence-derived pull + non-ratcheting
conviction. No bounded confidence, repulsion, or new network
mechanics in this experiment — those escalate only if magnitude-only
cells fail the full joint criteria.

## Registered findings this design rests on (IT2 155d008/fe1be1f)

1. Persistence has two independent fast-erasure mechanisms: pull
   alone (KA2, η=0: tau≈3d) and push alone (IT2 low-relax: tau
   2–3d). Either violates T1 by itself.
2. Weak push (η ≤ 0.05) cures homogenization (unanimity 0.001–0.005
   vs incumbent 0.134 and low-relax 1.00).
3. Conviction rails independently of homogenization.
4. Durable person-to-person transmission is absent in the incumbent
   system (T7 defect of the production law itself).

## Instrumentation (per cell)

IT3 instruments (per-pass contraction, homogenization panel every
10 days, day-90 paired tau fork) PLUS the registered trade-off
triple, measured together in every cell:
- PERSISTENCE: tau half-life + day-30 residual (ECONOMICS +0.15);
- TRANSMISSION: CULTURE +0.30 on 5k seeds; ring-1 (direct
  neighbors) and ring-2 (neighbors-of-neighbors, excluding seeds and
  ring-1) uptake at day 30;
- DIVERSITY: sd ratio, unanimity, saturation, within-country sd;
- CONVICTION two-sidedness: α snapshot at day 60 vs day 90 — the
  fraction of agents whose α DECREASED must be ≥ 10% (softening is
  real, not theoretical).

## Registered cell acceptance (ALL simultaneously)

- persistence: tau ∈ [5, 15]d, residual ∈ [0.2, 0.6];
- diversity: sd_ratio ≥ 0.5; unanimity < 50%; saturation < 20% per
  channel; no railing trend days 90→120;
- transmission: ring-1 uptake ∈ [0.006, 0.15] (measurable, not
  instant consensus) AND ring-2 uptake ≥ 0.0005 (spreads beyond);
- conviction: α mean ∈ [0.2, 0.9], sd ≥ 0.05, frac>0.99 < 20%,
  frac<0.05 < 20%, median (1−α) ≥ 0.10, softening fraction ≥ 10%;
- averaging/social term alive: per-pass contraction ≠ 1.0000 (the
  operator acts) with net daily variance contraction ≤ 2%.

Registered failure semantics: diversity-by-isolation FAILS
(transmission floor), transmission-by-homogenization FAILS
