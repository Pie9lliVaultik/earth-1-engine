**MAKE EARTH-1 WORK.**

Use all admissible TRAIN and DEVELOPMENT evidence aggressively to
improve predictive performance. Optimize until registered development
and validation targets are green. Do not preserve weak architecture for
historical reasons. Replace or extend components when evidence
identifies them as bottlenecks. Parallelize candidate solutions. Prefer
experiments that both diagnose failure and produce an implementable
improvement. The purpose of scientific controls is to prevent false
success, not to prevent optimization. HOLDOUT and PROSPECTIVE evidence
remain sacred: no tuning, model selection, feature selection, or
architecture decisions from those outcomes. Everything legitimately
designated TRAIN/DEVELOPMENT/VALIDATION exists to help us build the
strongest possible model. Success is the engineering objective.
Falsification is the final judge.
