The claim

The absence is the achievement.

Every other synthetic-population system answers a question by prompting a language model once per persona. That approach inherits the model's opinions, costs a fortune at scale, and — the part that actually matters — cannot be held to account. You cannot freeze a prompted answer, hash it, seal it, and score it six months later, because it will not reproduce.

Earth-1 took the harder road. The population is a data structure. The eight forces are arithmetic over that structure. A question is answered by branching the world, running real days, and measuring what changed against a control that got the same dice. There is nothing in the loop to ask, and nothing to be persuaded by.

We are not claiming Earth-1 guesses survey answers better than a frontier model.

We are claiming it is the only system of its kind that can be wrong on the record — and that it is already right about the things hardest to fake.

The remaining distance

It fails in six nameable ways — the rarest property a model can have.

A model that is diffusely wrong is dead. A model whose every miss resolves to one absent, buildable mechanism is a model with a roadmap. Every gap below was diagnosed from measurement, not guessed, and four of the six already have their public data fetched and hashed on disk.

no unemployment insurance Unemployment runs 2× over. The operator is specified and unbuilt.
no subsidies or substitution The 2022 food shock overshot 8.5×. Reality supplied the calibration target: Egypt's real change was exactly zero — the bread subsidy absorbed the entire shock.
no interbank plumbing Silicon Valley Bank was under-called at 0.302 against a resolved yes. The model put the United States as top force mover at twice the runner-up: right about the fear, missing the pipes.
no bond or pension channel The Truss gilt crisis is invisible — at full fidelity the shocked branch is indistinguishable from control. A clean, publishable negative result.
no religiosity input Religion is the worst-scoring opinion family at 3.91 sampling errors. The external, non-survey source is already parsed for 189 countries.
no geopolitical alignment input International-relations questions are the worst Pew family. UN voting ideal points for 178 countries are fetched, hashed, and waiting to be wired.
The line we do not cross

Earth-1 has never been benchmarked against a frontier language model, and never will be — it is prohibited by our own governing document, written before any results existed. We do not claim to be more accurate than a model at guessing what a survey said. We claim something that no prompted system can claim back: every number here was frozen before its judge was fetched, and can be re-derived, bit for bit, by anyone holding the seed.
