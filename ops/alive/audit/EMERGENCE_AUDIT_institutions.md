# CLUSTER 5 — INSTITUTIONS AND DEMOGRAPHY: designed vs. emergent

Scope: `earth1/institutions.py`, `generational.py`, `rebirth.py`, `rehome.py`, `plasticity.py`, audited against their live-path callers (`earth1/alive.py`) and their upstream sources (`earth1/genesis.py`, `earth1/life.py`, `earth1/fabric.py`).

**Load-bearing structural fact established first:** `generational.py::generational_tick` is **not in the live world**. `live_one_day` calls only `advance_age` (`alive.py:256-257`) for the clock, `health_tick` for mortality, and `_be_born` → `rebirth.apply_rebirth` for births (`alive.py:553`, `alive.py:620-621`). `generational_tick` is reached only from `g5.py:666`, `advance.py:52`, `legacy_answer.py:248`. Every demographic claim in `generational.py`'s docstring therefore describes a **parallel, non-production demographic authority**, and its inheritance geometry contradicts the live one (see §Prose).

## Prose

### The strongest genuinely-emergent findings in this cluster

**1. War is self-exciting, not Poisson.** `apply_policy_and_war` pushes FEAR `+0.03/day` on every person in a belligerent country (`institutions.py:279-280`); `govern` builds `unrest` from mean FEAR (`:155`) and multiplies war-onset risk by `(1 + 3·unrest)` (`:194`); rising unrest simultaneously drains legitimacy (`:186-189`), which multiplies risk again by `(1 + 2·(1−legitimacy))`. Neither the clustering nor the recurrence is written anywhere — they fall out of the coupling between the war-effects module and the onset formula. This is the cleanest institutional emergence here. The test is the dispersion of war-onset inter-arrival times against a Poisson null at the same mean.

**2. Directional selection on heritable setpoints.** Conception requires `life.relationship > 0.55` (`alive.py:604`) and `deprivation < 0.6` (`:605`); rebirth transmits `relationship_setpoint` as `parent + N(0,0.05)` (`rebirth.py:397-399`) and `wage` **exactly** (`rebirth.py:375`). Nothing sets a trend, yet the reproducing subpopulation is selected on heritable quantities, so mean partnership propensity and median wage among the living must drift upward across generations. This is genuine, unauthored, and measurable against a control with the gates removed.

**3. The genesis age–trait gradient decays.** `advance_age` deliberately omits trait aging (`generational.py:124-126`), while newborns enter *young* with traits blended toward the *all-ages* planetary mean (`rebirth.py:343-349`). Genesis wrote a real age gradient into every trait (`genesis.py:351-361`); the live demography erases it through turnover. Emergent, unintended, and directly relevant to any cohort/period claim the paper wants to make.

**4. Monotone homophilic densification of the friends layer.** Rewiring adds ~1.83·n mutual friend edges per year (`plasticity.py:121-123`), each selected to be force-close and therefore boosted to the weight ceiling and never pruned (`:106-107`, `:137`). Against a genesis friends layer of ~5·n edges this is a structural drift with no equilibrium: the network densifies and segregates without bound.

**5. Rebuilt tie degree degrades with world age.** `_sample_members` draws from all slots sharing a key and only afterwards filters the dead (`rehome.py:154-162` vs `:229`). Because unfilled dead slots accumulate, a migrant in year 50 rebuilds measurably fewer neighbours than one in year 1 — an emergent artefact of the sampling order.

### The most misleading docstring claims

**A. "Diaspora ties are the corridor" (`institutions.py:20-23`).** There is no corridor. Destination is `rng.choice(argsort(country_dep)[:25])` (`institutions.py:343-345`) — uniform over the 25 calmest countries. The diaspora matrix is written by `rehome` and read by no migration code. Self-reinforcing chain migration, the module's headline claim about migration, is not implemented.

**B. "Which one it reaches for depends on how much legitimacy it has left" (`institutions.py:6-7`).** It depends on `tax`. Arithmetic replay of `institutions.py:165-172` shows a bistable map with two absorbing corners, and which corner a country reaches is fixed by whether its genesis tax draw (`:105`, `0.42 − 0.08·tier ± 0.05`) clears the hard 0.22 gate. ~79% of LIC countries fail it at birth and are permanently locked into repression before any dynamics run. Legitimacy sits at 0.50-0.65 against a 0.28 gate and is nearly never binding — and it cannot move much, because `institutions.py:186-189` combined with `:179-181` reduces to the closed form `L(t) = L(0) − Δdep_norm − 0.6·Δunrest_norm`, an affine function of two EMAs with no accumulation. **The "legitimacy budget" is not a budget, and the spend/repress outcome is a genesis draw, not a decision.** This is the same failure mode as the fat-tails claim: a distribution imposed at birth, narrated as a dynamical outcome.

**C. "Wealth compounds… this is why the top pulls away instead of drifting, and it needs no conspiracy" (`institutions.py:326-329`).** The top pulls away because line 330 says `wealth[wealth > 30] *= 1.00012`. A threshold-gated exponential is not an explanation of concentration; it is concentration, hard-coded.

**D. Nuclear weapons (`institutions.py:52-68`, `:206-213`, `:256-258`).** `DETERRENCE_FACTOR` is labelled "war onset multiplier" but is applied only to the *target-selection weight* — nuclear states start wars at the base rate, so the world war count is invariant to nuclear armament. `NUCLEAR_USE_PER_WAR_YR` is declared, documented as a live risk in a comment, and **never read anywhere in the repository**. `CONSCRIPT_SHARE` (`:49`) is likewise a dead constant, and war "displaces people across borders" (`:17`) is implemented nowhere — there are no refugees.

**E. Two contradictory demographic authorities, and the paper-facing one is the worse of the pair.** `generational.py:266-268` and `:56-73` are built around a documented fix — anchor newborns to the **young cohort of their own country** and re-inject the country force residual, because the all-ages mean "erased that gradient every turnover". That module is **not in the live path**. The live birth operator does precisely the thing that fix was written to prevent: `rebirth.py:343-349` blends traits toward `mean(traits[all living agents worldwide])` and `:360-361` blends forces `0.5/0.5` toward the planetary mean with no noise. `rebirth.py:27-32` admits it ("a child in Niger inherits half a global average") and defers it. Consequence for the inequality question the audit asked: **between-country trait and force structure is systematically destroyed by the live birth operator, while wage, education, income and rent are transmitted perfectly with zero mobility (`rebirth.py:375`, `:328-332`, `:382-385`).** Long-run inequality in this engine is not grown — the income component is copied forward unchanged from the genesis draw at `life.py:336`, and the wealth component is re-drawn each generation from `wealth = 0` under a hand-set compounding constant. There is no mechanism anywhere in the engine that can create new mass in the wage tail.

**F. `plasticity.py:7-8` "this is how echo chambers form and bridges thin."** The rewire rule selects the nearest of 8 candidates in force space and the weight law rewards agreement — segregation is written into the update, not discovered by it. Worth stating precisely in the paper: the *magnitude* of the resulting assortativity is emergent from the coupling with the influence dynamics; the *direction* is designed. The honest control is `REWIRE_CANDIDATES = 1`.

### Two defects that will corrupt paper figures if unnoticed

- `api/readouts.py:394` publishes `unrest_norm` (the 500-day habituation baseline) under the field name `"unrest"`. Actual unrest is never stored.
- `rehome.py:142` clamps the **entire** tie matrix, not the added edges. The first hire day and the first migration day silently flatten colleague/neighbour/weak/diaspora weights to their nominal TIE_SPEC constants, destroying the genesis duplicate-stacking that `plasticity.py:103-105` documents and that `plasticity.py:150-152` explicitly refuses to touch for that exact reason.
