# CLUSTER 2 — MATERIAL LIFE: designed vs. emergent audit

**Scope:** `earth1/life.py`, `earth1/flourishing.py`, `earth1/health.py`, `earth1/weather.py`.
**Physics assumed:** the *deployed* configuration, not the module defaults. `earth1/configstamp.py:14-22` and `scripts/env/freeze09.env` define freeze-0.9 as `HARDSHIP_MODE=gradient`, `INCOME_CALIBRATION=v1`, `SUBSTRATE_FLAG=c2plus_v1`, `MORTALITY_MODE=gompertz`, `WANT_MODE=rr`, `DISTRESS_LAYOFFS=on`, plus `GM_OTHER_SHARE=0.2141`, `WANT_RR=4.9338`, `WEATHER_SCALE=0.0203`, `LAYOFF_GAIN=0.0028`. Every module default in this cluster (`cliff`, `off`, `legacy`) is *not* the physics any published number was produced under (`earth1/configstamp.py:1-11` says so explicitly). Classifications below are for freeze-0.9; where the flag changes the verdict I say so.

## Strongest genuinely-emergent findings in this cluster

1. **The fall→decline→fall cascade** (`health.py:226-246` → `health.py:211-212` → `weather.py:145-147`). A fall after age 60 permanently raises `declining`, which raises the fall hazard (×3.5 at full), raises weather-death frailty, removes the agent from the labour force forever, and increases isolation. Nothing about "who is in decline" is drawn or parameterised; it is a self-reinforcing loop crossing three modules. This is the cleanest emergent object in the cluster and it is defensible as such.
2. **Correlated layoffs reaching the social graph.** Firm assignment (`life.py:350-360`) is read directly into colleague ties (`fabric.py:152-155`), so a firm failure (`life.py:459-463`) simultaneously destitutes a set of agents *and* saturates each other's `shared` hardship term (`life.py:789,798-800`). No single component sets this; it requires firm structure × graph construction × the deprivation formula × the COLLECTIVE level map. **Important caveat for the paper:** the effect decays over time, because re-employment matching is uniform (`life.py:538-541`) and progressively destroys the Zipf firm-size distribution drawn at genesis. The mechanism is real; its strength is a function of world age and must be reported as such.
3. **Spell over-dispersion from two coupled hazards.** `spells` starts at zero for everyone (`life.py:396`) and is produced entirely by the dynamics; the state dependence is genuine and doubled — tenure resets to zero on loss, raising the separation hazard to 2.5× (`life.py:469`), while scarring cuts the finding hazard (`life.py:523`). Precarity, and hence FEAR, is therefore an accumulated biography, not a draw.
4. **Within-cohort mortality selection.** Under freeze-0.9 the *level* and *age curve* of death are imposed, but the RR (`health.py:309-311`) genuinely selects on deprivation, illness and addiction, none of which is targeted. The publishable claim is narrow and precise: *the engine produces a deprivation–mortality gradient within age strata that was not fitted.* Nothing wider than that survives the normalisation at `health.py:336-340`.
5. **Weather-death selection** (`weather.py:139-147`): who dies in a heat wave is a real interaction of age × illness × deprivation × post-fall decline, on a place-correlated exposure. The level is fitted; the selection is not.

## The most misleading docstring claims found

1. **`weather.py:27-29` — "anomalies are generated as a slow spatial field so neighbouring countries share weather, because they do."** The code draws i.i.d. Gaussian shocks per country with no spatial structure whatsoever (`weather.py:114`). Flatly false.
2. **`weather.py:58-61` — "adaptation is why 30C is deadly in Oslo and ordinary in Lagos."** Implemented as `comfort = baseline_temp` (`weather.py:88`), which makes heat/cold exposure statistically *identical* in every country. The docstring describes a mechanism whose implementation cancels it exactly.
3. **`health.py:23-26` — "the same tumour is survivable in Stockholm and fatal in Niamey."** Under the deployed `MORTALITY_MODE=gompertz`, disease resolution never kills (`health.py:277-282`); `TREATMENT_ACCESS`, `SURVIVE_TREATED`, `SURVIVE_UNTREATED` and the wealth-buys-care term are computed and thrown away, and `in_treatment` is read by nothing but the API. The sharpest inequality the module advertises has zero effect on any outcome.
4. **`life.py:120-128` — housing as "the main road to the street."** Rent never enters the budget (`life.py:575-577`, where the computed `housing` variable is discarded), so rent magnitude cannot cause arrears; and `evicted`/`arrears` are read by no dynamics at all — homelessness is decided from wealth and isolation in a different module (`institutions.py:303-312`).
5. **`life.py:38-40` — "Nothing here is tuned to produce a result."** Under freeze-0.9 the wage level and log-sd are fitted to fetched World Bank moments (`life.py:181-196` + `data/income_calibration.c2plus_v1.json`), the layoff gain is swept, `GM_OTHER_SHARE`, `WANT_RR` and `WEATHER_SCALE` are all fitted constants, and `INFORMAL_SCALE`/`HARDSHIP_GAIN` are declared calibratable.
6. **`life.py:456-458` — firm failure produces "a WAVE rather than a trickle."** Contradicted 300 lines earlier in the same file (`life.py:154-158`), which records the measurement that the hazard tops out at 2× and a covid-scale scenario destroyed −24±671 jobs. The code supports the second statement.
7. **`life.py:371-376` — setpoints "calibrated so an untouched population sits at GBD's ~13%."** The genesis draw `beta(8,2.2)` puts P(mental<0.45) at ≈1.5% against the module's own prevalence statistic (`life.py:738`).
8. **All "the first version did X and it produced Y" comments** (`life.py:80-85`, `99-101`, `567-574`, `615-618`, `621-628`, `867-872`, `884-894`; `flourishing.py:70-83`, `166-171`; `weather.py:42-46`, `108-113`, `119-122`; `health.py:259-262`). Each is a historical measurement claim about superseded code and must be classified **ASSERTED**; none can be verified from the current source, and several (the unemployment steady state, the destitution rate, the deprivation bimodality) describe parameters rather than results.

## Distributions: drawn at genesis vs produced by the dynamics

**Drawn and never overwritten (designed, persists for life):** wage (`life.py:335-336`, fitted moments), occupation (`life.py:325`), home ownership and rent (`life.py:381-388`), mental and relationship setpoints (`life.py:390-392`), country/age/education/urban/openness (all from `genesis.py`), initial wealth's tail (`life.py:366` — inherits the wage tail), initial firm-size Zipf (`life.py:359-360`, subsequently degraded).

**Drawn and erased within days–weeks (the draw is not the phenomenon):** hunger and thirst (`flourishing.py:116-117`; relax in ≤3 days and ≤2 days respectively), `physical` and its genesis age gradient (`life.py:410-411`; saturates at 1.0 in 2–4 weeks), firm health (`life.py:361`; OU with a ~250-day relaxation to a stationary sd ≈0.22 around 0.8).

**Zero at genesis, produced entirely by the dynamics (emergent):** `spells`, `deprivation` (as *state* — but see the caveat that its cross-section is close to a deterministic threshold on the drawn wage), `arrears`/`evicted` (inert), `n_events`, `declining`/`falls`, `art_received`, `lifetime_joy`, `days_homeless`, `cause_of_death`.

**Imposed by fitted external data (designed, and the strongest correction to any emergence claim):** the age-at-death distribution and crude death rate (`health.py:136-143`, `health.py:341`, `data/gompertz_world.v1.json`), the genesis age pyramid (`genesis.py:99-149`), the weather- and want-death shares (`weather.py:158`, `health.py:327`), and the wage level/dispersion (`life.py:196-197`).
