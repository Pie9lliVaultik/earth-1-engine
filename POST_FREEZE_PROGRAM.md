## The diagnosis, in one paragraph

The frozen engine implements steps 1 and 4 of the loop. Step 2
(differential impact) is absent: events apply one national force-delta
identically to every agent — a uniform shove on a population is
mathematically the same as shoving the mean (measured: variance ratio
0.16, rank corr −0.41, F1-under-B −0.08pp). Step 3 (nonlinear
interaction) is numerically dead: thresholds sit outside the reachable
state envelope (FEAR max 0.602 vs 0.7) and coupling has no cross-tick
memory, so the system runs linear and its aggregate is the aggregate
of its inputs. The population is rank-18 in information per country —
you cannot extract signal that was never injected. And the metric
(one number per country per question) is single-channel: it could not
see steps 2–3 even if they worked. The engine faithfully computes a
model in which news moves countries and agents are bookkeeping. The
target concept is a model in which news moves people and countries
are bookkeeping. Different physics; today's negatives are the
measurement proving which one is running.

   every dead or harmful component has authored ones (thresholds 0.7,
   feedback 0.02, coupling).
